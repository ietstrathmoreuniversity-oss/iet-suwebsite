A Proud Partnership Between IET & Strathmore University

For over a decade, Strathmore University has stood for academic rigour and ethical leadership in East Africa. The Institution of Engineering and Technology brings a global community of 168,000+ engineers across 148 countries. Together, our chapter gives Strathmore students a direct line into the world's largest professional engineering body — chartered status, technical journals, mentorship, and a passport to the global profession.

About the IET

A global home for
working engineers.

The Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET) is one of the world's largest engineering institutions — chartered in the United Kingdom, recognised across the globe, and home to a community committed to making engineering work for people.

Our chapter at Strathmore is the East African gateway. Joining gives you:

  • A direct path to Chartered (CEng) and Incorporated Engineer status
  • Free access to IET technical journals, e-books, and the IET Library
  • Mentorship from working engineers across 148 countries
  • Travel and project grants — including the IET Engineering Horizons Bursary
  • A line on your CV that hiring managers from Nairobi to London actually recognise
